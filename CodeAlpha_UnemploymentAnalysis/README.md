\# Unemployment Analysis in India



\## CodeAlpha Data Science Internship – Task 2



\### Project Overview

This project explores unemployment trends in India using Python and data visualization techniques. It investigates unemployment rates across regions, rural and urban areas, and the period surrounding the COVID-19 lockdown.



\### Objectives

\- Analyze unemployment trends over time.

\- Compare unemployment rates across Indian states.

\- Study rural and urban unemployment differences.

\- Examine unemployment patterns before and during the COVID-19 lockdown period.

\- Generate meaningful insights through data visualization.



\### Dataset

\*\*Dataset:\*\* Unemployment in India  

\*\*Source:\*\* \[Kaggle – Unemployment in India](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/gokulrajkmv/unemployment-in-india)

\### Analysis Performed

1\. Data loading and preprocessing.

2\. Missing-value and duplicate analysis.

3\. Unemployment rate distribution.

4\. Monthly unemployment trend analysis.

5\. State-wise unemployment comparison.

6\. Rural vs urban unemployment analysis.

7\. COVID-19 period comparison.

8\. Final insights and visualizations.
